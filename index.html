Who we are

Security built from the adversary’s point of view.

Founded as a vulnerability research firm, Vazkeft exists for one reason: the systems that national security and critical infrastructure depend on are attacked by professionals — and should be tested, defended, and built by professionals who think the same way.

We bring research and engineering together to help mission teams understand what is exposed, what matters, and how to make it stronger.

How we operate

Principles we don’t
trade away.

A direct, evidence-led approach from the first conversation through delivery.

01

Offense informs defense

Every defensive recommendation we make is grounded in vulnerabilities we have personally found and exploitation we have personally performed. We defend against what works, not what is marketed.

02

Evidence over theater

No severity-score hand-waving, no compliance box-checking dressed up as security. Findings ship with proof, root cause, and a fix — or they do not ship.

03

Engineering, not resale

We are not a reseller with a services arm. We build harnesses, detections, and pipelines from first principles, and everything we build in your environment belongs to you.

04

Small team, deep bench

Vazkeft stays deliberately small. The researcher who scopes your engagement is the one who executes it — and the one who answers the phone afterward.
